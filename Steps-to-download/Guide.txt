How to download phishguard

step 1: click the Try Now Button to download the app and after getting the the download page, click the download icon

step 2: continue downloading the apk file

step 3: after that, go the google play store and tap you profile

step 4: Under your profile, click the option Play Protect

step 5: click the Settings Icon

step 6: Toggle the 2 buttons

Step 7: Turn off the Play Protect for now, after installing the apk you can turn it on once again

Step 8: Go to your file manager in the Downloads folder and continue installing the apk, and Enjoy using it!
