How to download PhishGuard.

These steps are based on the provided guide and paired with the matching screenshots for easier setup.

1

Tap Try Now and download

Tap the Try Now button on the PhishGuard page. When the download page opens, tap the download icon for the APK file.

2

Continue the APK download

Confirm the browser download prompt so the PhishGuard APK can be saved to your device.

3

Open your Play Store profile

After the APK finishes downloading, open Google Play Store and tap your profile icon.

4

Go to Play Protect

From the profile menu, choose Play Protect so you can review the Android security settings for the install.

5

Open Play Protect settings

Tap the settings icon in the Play Protect screen to see the available protection toggles.

6

Adjust the two toggles

If Android blocks the APK install, temporarily switch off the two Play Protect toggles shown in the screenshot.

7

Confirm temporarily

Confirm the Play Protect prompt for now. After PhishGuard is installed, return here and turn Play Protect back on.

8

Install from Downloads

Open your file manager, go to the Downloads folder, tap the PhishGuard APK, and finish the installation.
